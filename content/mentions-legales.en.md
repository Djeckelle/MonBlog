## Personal data and cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own and contains no advertising tools. There is no form and no newsletter: you can follow new publications through the RSS feed, without signing up.

Three third-party services may be called while you browse:

- **Audience measurement** relies on GoatCounter, an open-source tool that sets no cookies. It counts page views and derives aggregate statistics from them (pages read, referring site, browser…); according to its publisher, it keeps neither the IP address nor any other data that could identify a visitor. Its only purpose is to know which content is read. Ad blockers often disable it, and that is perfectly fine.
- **Comments** at the bottom of articles rely on Giscus and GitHub Discussions. Leaving a comment requires a GitHub account and is subject to GitHub's terms.
- **The online dossier and the profile page** load their fonts from Google Fonts, which sends the visitor's IP address to Google.
