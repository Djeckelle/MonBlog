## Architecture & organisation {#architecture}

**Books**

- **Gregor Hohpe**, *The Software Architect Elevator* (O'Reilly, 2020). The founding metaphor of my practice: the architect rides between the boardroom and the engine room to keep intent and reality connected. A job of movement, not position.
- **Gregor Hohpe & Bobby Woolf**, *Enterprise Integration Patterns* (Addison-Wesley, 2003). Beyond the technique, a truth that runs through all my work: value — and fragility — live at the interfaces.
- **Murat Erder, Pierre Pureur, Eoin Woods**, *Continuous Architecture in Practice* (Addison-Wesley, 2021), preceded by *Continuous Architecture* (Erder & Pureur, Morgan Kaufmann, 2015). Architecture decided in small, reversible increments, anchored in quality attributes rather than in the technology of the day. → [continuous-architecture.org](https://www.continuous-architecture.org/)
- **Svyatoslav Kotusev**, *The Practice of Enterprise Architecture* (SK Publishing). Enterprise architecture as it is actually practised, far from theoretical frameworks — and the dialogue zone between the intentional and the emergent. → [kotusev.com](https://kotusev.com)
- **Matthew Skelton & Manuel Pais**, *Team Topologies* (IT Revolution, 2019). Cognitive load as a criterion for team boundaries, and designing the organisation for the target architecture — Conway in reverse.

**Texts and references**

- **Melvin Conway**, *How Do Committees Invent?* (Datamation, 1968). A system mirrors the communication structure of the organisation that designs it. [Original text](http://www.melconway.com/Home/Committees_Paper.html) · cited in [Value is at the interfaces](/en/articles/2026-06-24-valeur-aux-interfaces/), [Conway's Law and HR titles](/en/articles/2026-07-31-architect-conway-titles/)
- **Meir Lehman & László Belády**, laws of software evolution (from 1974). Without deliberate effort, a system's complexity grows. [Lehman's summary (PDF)](https://www.cs.kent.edu/~jmaletic/cs63902/Papers/Lehman96.pdf) · cited in [Technical debt is carbon debt](/en/articles/2026-09-11-la-dette-technique-est-une-dette-carbone/)
- **Vitruvius**, *De architectura* (1st century BC). *Firmitas, utilitas, venustas*: the triad still holds. [Overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_architectura) · cited in [Everyone optimises, no one arbitrates](/en/articles/2026-09-04-chacun-optimise-personne-arbitre/)
- **Xavier Chaumont**, *Enterprise Architecture — What or Why in 7 images* (LinkedIn, 2024). Architecture as a bridge and as a game of chess. [Profile](https://fr.linkedin.com/in/xchaumont/) · cited in [Everyone optimises, no one arbitrates](/en/articles/2026-09-04-chacun-optimise-personne-arbitre/)

## Artificial intelligence: understand, demystify, govern {#ai}

**Books**

- **Laurence Poussard**, *Nous venons du chaos, l'IA de la logique* (2025, in French). My book: AI is neither devil nor messiah, but a lever of influence in the hands of those who set its rules. [On Amazon](https://amzn.eu/d/07jFAfN9)
- **Luc Julia**, *L'intelligence artificielle n'existe pas* (First, 2019) and *IA génératives, pas créatives* (Le Cherche Midi, 2025), in French. Demystification by one of Siri's creators: statistical, not intelligent; imitative, not creative.
- **Melanie Mitchell**, *Artificial Intelligence: A Guide for Thinking Humans* (FSG, 2019). The most balanced guide to understanding before judging — neither doom nor wonder.
- **Brian Christian**, *The Alignment Problem* (Norton, 2020). "Which values do we encode?" is not an abstract question: it is already, technically, at work.
- **Arvind Narayanan & Sayash Kapoor**, *AI Snake Oil* (Princeton, 2024). What AI can do versus what it is claimed to do — especially "predictive" AI. A rigorous antidote to hype.
- **Nick Bostrom**, *Superintelligence* (Oxford, 2014). The classic on existential risk. I read it at a distance: thinking about the worst does not require believing in it.
- **Karen Hao**, *Empire of AI* (Penguin, 2025). The real power struggles behind the AI race — a counterpoint to the stories labs tell about themselves.
- **William El Kaim**, *AI-Augmented Enterprise Architecture* (2026). What AI changes in the architect's job, with a public metamodel. [AI-Augmented EA Codex](https://eacodex.ai/) · cited in [Cure and poison](/en/articles/2026-10-14-remede-et-poison-posologie-ia/)

**Sources**

- **Mustafa Suleyman**, *Towards Humanist Superintelligence* (Microsoft AI, 2025). A stated intent to set against the reality of execution. [Manifesto](https://microsoft.ai/news/towards-humanist-superintelligence/) · cited in [Strategic intent × anthropological reality](/en/articles/2026-01-15-intention-strategique-realite-anthropologique/)
- **Nirit Weiss-Blatt**, *AI Panic* (newsletter). How the catastrophist narrative is built, and who benefits. [aipanic.news](https://www.aipanic.news/) · cited in [When AI plays at scaring itself](/en/articles/2026-06-03-quand-l-ia-joue-a-se-faire-peur/)
- **Leo XIV**, encyclical *Magnifica humanitas* (Vatican, 2026), and **Christopher Olah's speech** (Anthropic) at its presentation: a lab co-founder acknowledging that these companies are trapped by their incentives. [Encyclical](https://www.vatican.va/content/leo-xiv/fr/encyclicals/documents/20260515-magnifica-humanitas.html) · [Speech](https://www.anthropic.com/news/chris-olah-pope-leo-encyclical) · cited in [When AI plays at scaring itself](/en/articles/2026-06-03-quand-l-ia-joue-a-se-faire-peur/)

## Cognition, work & mental load {#work}

- **Sparrow, Liu, Wegner**, *Google Effects on Memory* (Science, 2011). Knowing information stays accessible reduces the effort to remember it. [Study](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1207745) · cited in [What AI does to our brain](/en/articles/2026-06-17-ce-que-ia-fait-a-notre-cerveau/)
- **Lee et al.**, *The Impact of Generative AI on Critical Thinking* (Microsoft Research & Carnegie Mellon, 2025). The more we rely on AI, the less we exercise critical thinking. [Study](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/the-impact-of-generative-ai-on-critical-thinking-self-reported-reductions-in-cognitive-effort-and-confidence-effects-from-a-survey-of-knowledge-workers/) · cited in [What AI does to our brain](/en/articles/2026-06-17-ce-que-ia-fait-a-notre-cerveau/)
- **Søren Dinesen Østergaard**, editorials on chatbots and delusion (Schizophrenia Bulletin, 2023; Acta Psychiatrica Scandinavica, 2025). A hypothesis in 2023, documented cases in 2025. [2023](https://academic.oup.com/schizophreniabulletin/article/49/6/1418/7251361) · [2025](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/acps.70022) · cited in [Strategic intent × anthropological reality](/en/articles/2026-01-15-intention-strategique-realite-anthropologique/)
- **Monique Haicault**, "La gestion ordinaire de la vie en deux" (Sociologie du travail, 1984), the founding text on mental load, and her history of the notion (2020), in French. [1984](https://hal.science/halshs-01503920/) · [2020](https://hal.science/hal-02881589) · cited in [What if AI gave the mind back to what matters?](/en/articles/2026-09-26-rendre-l-esprit-a-l-essentiel/)
- **Marcus Raichle et al.**, "A default mode of brain function" (PNAS, 2001), and **Roger Beaty et al.** on predicting creative ability (PNAS, 2018). The resting brain is at work, and creativity depends on it. [Raichle](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.98.2.676) · [Beaty](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1713532115) · cited in [What if AI gave the mind back to what matters?](/en/articles/2026-09-26-rendre-l-esprit-a-l-essentiel/)
- **Aiken et al.**, "Hospital Nurse Staffing and Patient Mortality" (JAMA, 2002). Caregiver overload is measured in lives. [Study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12387650/) · cited in [What if AI gave the mind back to what matters?](/en/articles/2026-09-26-rendre-l-esprit-a-l-essentiel/)
- **Bellet, De Neve, Ward**, "Does Employee Happiness Have an Impact on Productivity?" (Management Science, 2024). Yes, and it has been measured. [Study](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2023.4766) · cited in [What if AI gave the mind back to what matters?](/en/articles/2026-09-26-rendre-l-esprit-a-l-essentiel/)
- **WHO**, *Mental health at work*; **Fondation Jean-Jaurès / Ifop**, *Je t'aime, moi non plus* (2023, in French); **Randstad**, *Workmonitor 2025*. The cost of saturation and the new relationship to work. [WHO](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/mental-health-at-work) · [Jean-Jaurès](https://www.jean-jaures.org/publication/je-taime-moi-non-plus-les-ambivalences-du-nouveau-rapport-au-travail/) · [Randstad](https://www.randstad.com/press/2025/work-life-balance-tops-pay-randstads-workmonitor-reveals/) · cited in [What if AI gave the mind back to what matters?](/en/articles/2026-09-26-rendre-l-esprit-a-l-essentiel/)
- **Sawhney et al.**, "A meta-analytic review of 60 years of role stressor research" (Journal of Vocational Behavior, 2026). Role ambiguity, the leading source of workplace stress. [Study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jvb.2026.104234) · cited in [When the Unknown Pushes Us to Do Other People's Work](/en/articles/2026-07-31-travail-des-autres-ecosysteme/)
- **Cross, Rebele, Grant**, "Collaborative Overload" (Harvard Business Review, 2016), and **Gallup**, *State of the Global Workplace*. Collaborative overload and unclear expectations. [HBR](https://hbr.org/2016/01/collaborative-overload) · [Gallup](https://www.gallup.com/workplace/349484/state-of-the-global-workplace.aspx) · cited in [When the Unknown Pushes Us to Do Other People's Work](/en/articles/2026-07-31-travail-des-autres-ecosysteme/)

## Sustainable IT {#sustainable-it}

- **International Energy Agency**, *Energy and AI* and *Key Questions on Energy and AI*. The reference on datacentre and AI electricity demand. [Energy and AI](https://www.iea.org/reports/energy-and-ai/energy-demand-from-ai) · [Key Questions](https://www.iea.org/reports/key-questions-on-energy-and-ai/executive-summary) · cited in [AI Maximalism Is Not a Sign of Boldness](/en/articles/2026-07-27-pragmatisme-frugalite-ia/), [AI is not the arsonist](/en/articles/2026-09-09-ce-n-est-pas-l-ia-qui-est-pyromane/)
- **Energy Institute**, *Statistical Review of World Energy*. Another estimate, another scope — hence the gap with the IEA. [Report](https://www.energyinst.org/statistical-review) · cited in [AI is not the arsonist](/en/articles/2026-09-09-ce-n-est-pas-l-ia-qui-est-pyromane/)
- **William Stanley Jevons**, *The Coal Question* (1865), and **Antoine Missemer**, *Les Économistes et la fin des énergies fossiles* (Classiques Garnier, 2017, in French). The rebound effect, and its limits. [Overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Coal_Question) · cited in [AI is not the arsonist](/en/articles/2026-09-09-ce-n-est-pas-l-ia-qui-est-pyromane/)
- **ADEME / Arcep**, *Environmental footprint of digital technology in France* (2022, updated 2025), and the annual survey *Pour un numérique soutenable*. The French reference figures. [Arcep](https://www.arcep.fr/la-regulation/grands-dossiers-thematiques-transverses/lempreinte-environnementale-du-numerique.html) · [2025 survey](https://www.arcep.fr/cartes-et-donnees/nos-publications-chiffrees/impact-environnemental/enquete-annuelle-pour-un-numerique-soutenable-edition-2025.html) · cited in [Everyone optimises, no one arbitrates](/en/articles/2026-09-04-chacun-optimise-personne-arbitre/)
- **RGESN**, the French general reference framework for the eco-design of digital services (Arcep, Arcom, ADEME, 2024). [Framework](https://ecoresponsable.numerique.gouv.fr/publications/referentiel-general-ecoconception/) · cited in [Technical debt is carbon debt](/en/articles/2026-09-11-la-dette-technique-est-une-dette-carbone/)
- **ISO/IEC 21031:2024 (SCI)** and **ISO/IEC 30134-2 (PUE)**. Measuring software carbon intensity, and datacentre efficiency. [SCI](https://www.iso.org/standard/86612.html) · [Green Software Foundation](https://greensoftware.foundation/standards/sci/) · [PUE](https://www.iso.org/standard/30134-2) · cited in [Everyone optimises, no one arbitrates](/en/articles/2026-09-04-chacun-optimise-personne-arbitre/), [What is already manufactured is already paid for](/en/articles/2026-09-17-ce-qui-est-deja-fabrique-est-deja-paye/)
- **Jonathan Koomey & Jon Taylor**, work on "comatose" servers (Anthesis Group, from 2015). Nearly one server in three runs without producing anything. [Analysis](https://www.anthesisgroup.com/insights/zombie-servers-hunting-down-the-lost-capital/) · [Report (PDF)](https://info.anthesisgroup.com/hubfs/Website%20PDFs/Comatose-Servers-Redux.pdf) · cited in [Technical debt is carbon debt](/en/articles/2026-09-11-la-dette-technique-est-une-dette-carbone/)

## Philosophy of technology {#philosophy}

- **Plato**, *Phaedrus*. The myth of Theuth: writing as both cure and poison for memory.
- **Jacques Derrida**, "Plato's Pharmacy", in *Dissemination* (1972). The *pharmakon* as undecidable.
- **Bernard Stiegler**, *What Makes Life Worth Living: On Pharmacology* (2010; English translation Polity, 2013). Every technology is a pharmakon: the question is the dose, and who sets it.

All three cited in [Cure and poison: AI dosage is an architecture decision](/en/articles/2026-10-14-remede-et-poison-posologie-ia/).
