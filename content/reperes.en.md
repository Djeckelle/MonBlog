## The book

### *Nous venons du chaos, l'IA de la logique* ("We Come From Chaos, AI From Logic") (2025)

AI is neither the devil nor the messiah. It's a lever of massive influence — economic, political, cognitive — in the hands of those who build it and set its rules. My book (in French) defends a stance, neither doomer nor boomer, and a conviction: to move beyond apocalyptic and messianic discourse and give back the power to decide to those to whom it belongs.

## Analytical dossiers

### *Continuous Architecture Facing the Unpredictable* (2026)

My most complete dossier. It starts from an observation: **geopolitics has become an architecture variable in its own right.** Choosing a cloud, a model, a vendor is no longer a techno-economic trade-off — it's a bet on the stability of the world.

This dossier explores what traditional architectures fail to see, proposes the value loop of the strategic architect, six principles for handling complexity without denying it, a maturity radar, a mapping of geopolitical risks against continuous-architecture levers, and seven questions to put to your next executive committee.

A long, dense format — to read when you have time to think.

[Read the dossier →](/dossiers/architecture-continue-polycrise-2026.html)
