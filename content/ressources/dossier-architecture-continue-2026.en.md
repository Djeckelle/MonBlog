*Strategic analysis dossier · March 2026*

In 2026, the polycrisis is no longer a context in which an information system is deployed. It has become an architecture variable. This dossier examines what that changes — for enterprise architects, for executive leadership, for organisations that must now make their technology choices in a lastingly unstable environment.

[**Read the full dossier**](/dossiers/architecture-continue-polycrise-2026-en.html)

## What you will find in the dossier

The dossier is designed as a working tool, not an article to skim. It covers:

- **The new reality** — Why geopolitics is becoming an architecture variable, and the three shifts of 2026 that make this change permanent and cumulative
- **The blind spot** — What traditional enterprise architecture approaches fail to see, and why the broken translation between strategy and execution has become organisations' critical weakness
- **The value loop** — The four stages of the strategic architect's practice: capture, translate, structure, learn
- **The dialogue zone** — How Continuous Architecture concretely connects the strategic layer and the execution layer
- **Six principles** for a living practice, applicable at the scale of a large organisation
- **A maturity radar** in orders of magnitude from field observations — where large organisations really stand
- **A map** of seven critical geopolitical risks, their nature, their impact, and the appropriate architectural response
- **Three governance changes** executive committees need to move from displayed compliance to real resilience
- **Seven questions** to put to your next executive committee — the ones that make the decision possible and accountable

## Who this dossier is for

**Enterprise architects** who want to extend their practice beyond a product or domain scope and deploy it across the organisation.

**CIOs** who see that compliance no longer stands in for strategy, and who are looking for a framework to connect systems and geopolitics.

**HR directors** and **executive leadership** who want to understand why the enterprise architect's role has become strategic — and what that means for positioning, team composition and investment.

**Executive committee members** who want a rigorous reading of their organisation's technology dependence, and concrete questions to bring to their decision-making bodies.

## Format and length

The dossier can be read directly online. It is a standalone HTML page with an anchored side table of contents, interactive sections (value loop, dialogue zone, expandable principles, animated radar) and references.

**Estimated reading time**: 15 to 20 minutes straight through, longer if you stop on the interactive elements or the risk tables.
